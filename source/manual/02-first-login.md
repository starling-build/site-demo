# First login

Once you sign in, you land on the desktop:

- a **wallpaper** filling the screen,
- a thin [**menu bar**](#the-menu-bar) across the top, with the clock and
  status indicators,
- a [**dock**](#the-dock) floating at the bottom, holding your apps.

That is the whole desktop. Everything else is reached from the dock, the
[Launchpad](#the-launchpad), or a [right-click on the wallpaper](#right-click-the-desktop).

> **Tip.** The keyboard reaches most of it too. The whole list is in
> [Keyboard shortcuts](#keyboard-shortcuts); the four to learn first are
> **Ctrl + ↑** for Mission Control, **Ctrl + ←** / **→** to move between
> spaces, **Ctrl + ↓** for your workspace and **Esc** to back out of any of
> them.
